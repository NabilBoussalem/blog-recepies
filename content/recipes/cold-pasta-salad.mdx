ingredients:
  - "500g rotini or fusilli pasta"
  - "1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1 cucumber, diced"
  - "1/2 red bell pepper, diced"
  - "1/2 yellow bell pepper, diced"
  - "1/2 cup Kalamata olives, halved"
  - "1/4 cup red onion, finely diced"
  - "150g fresh mozzarella pearls"
  - "1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped"
  - "For the dressing:"
  - "1/3 cup olive oil"
  - "3 tbsp red wine vinegar"
  - "1 tsp Dijon mustard"
  - "1 clove garlic, minced"
  - "1 tsp dried oregano"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
instructions:
  - "Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain and rinse under cold water until completely cool."
  - "While pasta cooks, prepare all vegetables."
  - "Whisk together olive oil, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper."
  - "In a large bowl, combine cooled pasta with all vegetables, olives, and mozzarella."
  - "Pour dressing over the salad and toss to coat evenly."
  - "Add fresh basil and give a final toss."
  - "Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving."
  - "Taste and adjust seasoning before serving. Add a splash of vinegar if needed."

This **Cold Pasta Salad** is the ultimate make-ahead dish for summer gatherings. Packed with fresh veggies and dressed in a tangy vinaigrette, it gets even better as it sits.

<Tip>Rinse the pasta thoroughly under cold water to stop the cooking process and prevent it from getting mushy.</Tip>

## Make It Your Own

Feel free to customize with your favorite additions:
- Artichoke hearts
- Sun-dried tomatoes
- Pepperoncini
- Salami or prosciutto for a non-vegetarian version
