This **Cucumber Yogurt Salad** is the ultimate no-cook side dish for scorching summer days. Inspired by Mediterranean and Middle Eastern traditions, it comes together in minutes and tastes even better after chilling.

<Tip>Use English cucumbers for a crunchier texture with fewer seeds.</Tip>

## Why You'll Love It

- **No cooking required** — just chop and mix
- Refreshing and light
- High in protein from Greek yogurt
- Ready in under 15 minutes of active work

<NutritionBox calories={85} protein="5g" carbs="8g" fat="4g" fiber="1g" />

## Serving Suggestions

Pair with grilled pita bread, falafel, or alongside any grilled protein. It also makes a fantastic topping for grain bowls.

<Callout type="info">This salad keeps well in the fridge for up to 2 days. Give it a stir before serving.</Callout>
