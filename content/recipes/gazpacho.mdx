  - "Add olive oil and sherry vinegar. Blend again briefly."
  - "Season with salt and pepper to taste."
  - "Pass through a sieve for an extra-smooth texture (optional)."
  - "Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours."
  - "Serve cold, garnished with diced cucumber, a drizzle of olive oil, and fresh herbs."

This **Classic Gazpacho** is the crown jewel of cold summer soups. Originating from southern Spain, it's a raw, blended soup that showcases the best of summer tomatoes.

<Tip>Use the ripest tomatoes you can find — they make all the difference in flavor. In-season heirloom tomatoes work beautifully.</Tip>

## A Brief History

Gazpacho has been a staple of Spanish cuisine for centuries. Originally a humble peasant dish made from stale bread, garlic, olive oil, and vinegar, it evolved to include New World tomatoes and peppers.

## Variations to Try

- **White Gazpacho (Ajo Blanco):** Made with almonds, grapes, and garlic
- **Watermelon Gazpacho:** Add watermelon for a sweeter twist
- **Green Gazpacho:** Use tomatillos, cucumber, and herbs

<Callout type="info">Gazpacho tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld. It keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days.</Callout>
