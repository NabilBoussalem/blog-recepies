instructions:
  - "Add frozen mango, frozen banana, coconut milk, orange juice, and honey to a blender."
  - "Blend on high until thick and creamy. The consistency should be thicker than a regular smoothie."
  - "If too thick, add a splash more liquid. If too thin, add more frozen fruit."
  - "Pour into two bowls."
  - "Arrange toppings artfully: granola, sliced kiwi, blueberries, coconut flakes, and chia seeds."
  - "Drizzle with honey and serve immediately."

This **Mango Smoothie Bowl** brings tropical vibes to your breakfast table. Thick, creamy, and loaded with colorful toppings, it's almost too pretty to eat.

<Tip>For the thickest consistency, use frozen fruit straight from the freezer and minimal liquid.</Tip>

## Topping Ideas

Get creative with your toppings:
- Fresh seasonal fruit
- Nuts and seeds
- Nut butter drizzle
- Cacao nibs
- Bee pollen

<NutritionBox calories={290} protein="5g" carbs="58g" fat="7g" fiber="6g" />

<Callout type="info">For a vegan version, use maple syrup instead of honey and plant-based milk.</Callout>
