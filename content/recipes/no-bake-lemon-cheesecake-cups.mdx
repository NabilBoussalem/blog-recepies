ingredients:
  - "250g cream cheese, softened"
  - "1/2 cup powdered sugar"
  - "Zest of 2 lemons"
  - "3 tbsp fresh lemon juice"
  - "1 cup heavy whipping cream"
  - "1 tsp vanilla extract"
  - "1 cup graham cracker crumbs"
  - "3 tbsp melted butter"
  - "Fresh berries for topping"
  - "Mint leaves for garnish"
instructions:
  - "Mix graham cracker crumbs with melted butter until combined."
  - "Divide the mixture among 6 small cups or glasses, pressing down firmly."
  - "Beat cream cheese with powdered sugar until smooth."
  - "Add lemon zest, lemon juice, and vanilla extract. Beat until combined."
  - "In a separate bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form."
  - "Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture."
  - "Spoon the lemon filling over the graham cracker bases."
  - "Refrigerate for at least 2 hours until set."
  - "Top with fresh berries and mint leaves before serving."

These **No-Bake Lemon Cheesecake Cups** are sunshine in dessert form. Light, creamy, and bursting with fresh lemon flavor — no oven needed!

<Tip>For the smoothest texture, make sure your cream cheese is fully at room temperature before mixing.</Tip>

## Why No-Bake is Perfect for Summer

- No heating up the kitchen
- Minimal hands-on time
- Can be made a day ahead
- Individual portions — no slicing needed
