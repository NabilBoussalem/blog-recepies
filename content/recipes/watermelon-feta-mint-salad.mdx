ingredients:
  - "6 cups seedless watermelon, cubed"

  - "1/4 red onion, thinly sliced"
  - "2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1 tbsp balsamic glaze"
  - "Juice of 1 lime"
  - "Flaky sea salt and black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Cut the watermelon into bite-sized cubes and arrange on a serving platter."
  - "Scatter crumbled feta over the watermelon."
  - "Add thinly sliced red onion and torn mint leaves."
  - "Drizzle with olive oil, balsamic glaze, and lime juice."
  - "Season with flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper."
  - "Serve immediately for the best texture."

This **Watermelon Feta Mint Salad** is the quintessential summer dish. The contrast of sweet watermelon, salty feta, and aromatic mint is absolutely irresistible.

<Tip>Choose a ripe, seedless watermelon for the best results. It should feel heavy for its size and sound hollow when tapped.</Tip>

## Perfect For

- Summer barbecues and picnics
- Pool parties
- Light lunches
- Side dish for grilled meats

<NutritionBox calories={120} protein="4g" carbs="14g" fat="6g" fiber="1g" />

<Callout type="info">For a vegan version, swap feta for marinated tofu cubes.</Callout>
